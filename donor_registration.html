EMERGENCY PROTOCOL

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN SOMEONE NEEDS YOUR BLOOD?

A simple, reassuring look at how emergency coordination works when a critical requirement arises.

STEP 1

🚨 EMERGENCY REQUEST

A hospital, patient relative, or doctor submits a verified urgent blood requirement to LifeSavers United.

STEP 2

🩸 BLOOD GROUP MATCH

Our platform identifies compatible donor blood groups suitable for the requested component.

STEP 3

📍 LOCATION MATCH

Donors located within reasonable proximity to the hospital or blood bank are matched.

STEP 4

📲 AVAILABLE DONORS CONTACTED

Matched, eligible donors receive an emergency request notification with patient and hospital details.

STEP 5

🤝 YOU DECIDE

You review your personal availability and schedule, and freely choose whether you can respond.

STEP 6

💉 DONATION

You donate safely at the accredited hospital, authorized blood bank, or licensed collection facility.

Medical Note: LifeSavers United operates as an emergency matching and coordination network. All medical screenings, testing, blood collection, and transfusions are conducted exclusively by licensed medical practitioners at certified blood banks and authorized healthcare facilities.

COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear, straightforward answers about registration, donation eligibility, emergency coordination, and your privacy.

REGISTRATION
Any healthy adult between 18 and 65 years of age weighing at least 45 kg (or 50 kg for Single Donor Platelet donations) can register. You should not have any active serious medical conditions or recent major surgery.
Registration takes less than 2 minutes. Simply fill in your basic personal details, blood group, and location in the form above.
No. Blood donation is 100% voluntary. You always have full freedom to assess your physical readiness and availability whenever a coordinator contacts you.
DONATION
When a patient needs blood in your vicinity, a coordinator reaches out. If you agree, you visit the certified hospital blood bank or donation center, undergo a quick free health check (hemoglobin, blood pressure, weight), and complete your donation in about 10–15 minutes under licensed medical supervision.
For Whole Blood, male donors can donate every 90 days (3 months) and female donors every 120 days (4 months). For Single Donor Platelets (SDP), donors can safely donate every 14 days, up to a maximum of 24 times a year.
You rest for 10–15 minutes and enjoy fluids or light refreshments provided by the blood center. You can log your donation in your LifeSavers United Donor Portal to update your records, receive milestone certificates, and track your next eligible date.
EMERGENCY REQUESTS
You will be contacted via your chosen preferred contact method (WhatsApp, phone call, or SMS) by an authorized LifeSavers United coordinator when an emergency requires your blood group in your area.
Yes, absolutely. If you are unwell, traveling, busy, or otherwise unavailable, you can simply decline without any hesitation or penalty.
Yes. You can manage your availability, contact preferences, or activate rest mode through your Donor Portal or by reaching out to our helpline.
DONOR PORTAL
Visit the Donor Portal and enter your registered mobile number. Your complete history of past donations and dates will be displayed.
Your official Digital Donor Card is available under the "Digital Donor Card" tab in the Donor Portal. You can download it as a high-resolution PNG image or share it directly on WhatsApp.
The Donor Portal automatically calculates your mandatory medical interval based on DGHS guidelines and displays a clear countdown to your next eligible donation date.
Under the "Appreciation Certificate" tab in the Donor Portal, you can generate and download official verified LifeSavers United appreciation certificates recognizing your lifesaving contributions.
PRIVACY
Your information is strictly used to match you with patients requiring your specific blood type during medical emergencies and to provide you with your digital donor card and records.
No. Your personal contact number and medical history are never published publicly on the internet. Only verified emergency coordinators handle match outreach in accordance with our strict privacy policy.
