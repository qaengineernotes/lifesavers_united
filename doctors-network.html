HUMANITARIAN MISSION

DRIVEN BY COMPASSION, BACKED BY EXPERIENCE

For the past 3 years, LifeSavers United has operated as a purely voluntary initiative working on:

🩸

Emergency Blood Donation

Rapid response networks actively connecting voluntary blood donors to critical patients, surgeries, and emergency trauma victims across Ahmedabad, Gujarat, and pan-India 24/7.

🧬
