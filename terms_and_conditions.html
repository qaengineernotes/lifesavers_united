  • Official Information Sources & Regulatory Changes: The blood donation rules, donor deferral tables, and component interval criteria published on this platform are compiled from official guidelines issued by statutory bodies including the DGHS, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW), and the eRaktKosh National Portal. Official notifications issued by the DGHS/MoHFW supersede any content on this site, and users must verify and rely on the latest notifications directly on official statutory portals.

Users must directly call and verify details with the respective blood bank or hospital before traveling or making critical emergency decisions.

3. Emergency Matching & No Guarantee of Fulfillment

In life-or-death emergencies, time is of the essence. We mobilize all available broadcast and notification channels (WhatsApp, Telegram, website alerts, volunteer networks) to identify willing donors. However:

  • No Guaranteed Arrival: Because all donors are unpaid volunteers balancing personal and professional commitments, LifeSavers United cannot guarantee that a donor will respond, qualify medically, or arrive at the hospital in time.
  • Patient Attendant Duty: Requesters and patient families must continue pursuing all hospital blood bank, regional blood storage center, and institutional channels concurrently. Do not rely exclusively on LifeSavers United for critical emergency outcomes.
  • Mandatory Duty to Report Fulfillment: Once your blood or platelet requirement has been met—whether through our network, hospital blood reserves, or family donors—the patient attendant must immediately notify LifeSavers United via our helpline or WhatsApp. Prompt reporting stops active emergency broadcasts, prevents redundant mobilization, and ensures voluntary donors are available for other dying patients.

4. Strict Zero-Tolerance Policy: Buying/Selling Blood & Fraud Prevention

Under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and directives of the Supreme Court of India, commercial blood trade, paid donors, or broker syndicates are strictly illegal and punishable by imprisonment.

  • All blood donations coordinated through LifeSavers United must be 100% voluntary, altruistic, and free of financial compensation.
  • Any attempt by a donor to solicit money, conveyance gifts, or payments from a patient family will result in immediate permanent banning and formal reporting to law enforcement authorities.
  • Any commercial broker, agent, or tout attempting to use this platform to trade blood units will be subject to criminal prosecution.

⚠️ Urgent Warning: Beware of Scams & Impersonators

LifeSavers United, its trustees, coordinators, and volunteer team NEVER ask for money, delivery fees, transport charges, conveyance costs, or blood bag processing fees via cash, UPI, QR codes, or personal bank transfers. Neither volunteers nor donors are authorized to collect funds. If anyone claiming to represent LifeSavers United demands financial payment, refuse immediately and report them to our official helpline at +91 9979260393.

5. User Conduct, Ethics & Humanitarian Neutrality

By accessing or using our platform, you agree to adhere to strict ethical standards:

  • Honesty in Request Submissions: You agree that all details provided in an emergency request (patient name, required blood component, hospital, contact numbers) are authentic, accurate, and correspond to an actual admitted medical patient. Submitting hoax, fake, or prank requests is a criminal offense and will be handed over to cybercrime authorities.
  • Universal Humanitarian Neutrality & Non-Discrimination: Blood has no religion, caste, or race. LifeSavers United operates strictly on universal humanitarian principles without discrimination based on religion, caste, creed, gender, linguistic identity, or socio-economic background. Emergency appeals are prioritized purely by medical urgency and clinical compatibility.
  • Respect for Donors: Requesters agree not to harass, repeatedly cold-call, or abuse registered donors who are resting, unavailable, or medically deferred.
  • Data Protection & Anti-Spam: Donor and volunteer contact numbers shared during an emergency match must be used solely for coordinating that specific donation. Harvesting contact numbers for commercial promotions, marketing, or political broadcasting is strictly forbidden.

6. Voluntary Donor Health Guidelines & Consent

If you register as a voluntary blood donor, you confirm and agree that:

  • You are at least 18 years of age and weigh at least 45 kg, in compliance with DGHS guidelines. Minors are strictly prohibited from registering as donors.
  • You will disclose all relevant medical history truthfully to the blood bank doctor during your pre-donation health check.
  • You understand the mandatory inter-donation intervals (minimum 90 days for male whole blood donors, 120 days for female whole blood donors, and 48 hours for platelet/apheresis donations). For official, comprehensive interval schedules across all blood components and deferral criteria, please visit the official Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS), the eRaktKosh National Portal, or consult our detailed DGHS Blood Donation Rules Guide and interactive Health Assessment Tools.
  • You understand that donation is completely voluntary, and you may decline or postpone any emergency request at your discretion without penalty.

  • When an emergency blood request involves a minor patient (below 18 years of age), the person submitting the form represents and warrants that they are the lawful parent or legal guardian of the child, possessing full legal capacity to share necessary medical information for urgent donor matching.

8. Clinical Safety & Authorized Donation Venues

To ensure absolute physical and medical safety for both donors and patient families:

  • Authorized Venues Only: All blood collections, platelet apheresis, and clinical evaluations must take place exclusively inside licensed hospital premises, accredited blood banks, or official government-sanctioned mobile blood donation camps under certified medical supervision.
  • Prohibition of Private Locations: Under no circumstances should any blood donation, blood testing, or specimen collection occur in private residences, vehicles, or non-clinical environments.
  • Independent Citizen Interaction: Donors and patient attendants connect as independent private citizens. LifeSavers United does not escort, supervise, or arrange physical transport for donors or patient families.
