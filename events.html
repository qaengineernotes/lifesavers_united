Our Events & Activities

Every blood donation drive, awareness seminar, and volunteer initiative reflects our commitment to saving lives across Gujarat & India.

Empowering Communities Through Life-Saving Events

At LifeSavers United, events are at the heart of our mission to bridge the critical gap between blood donors, emergency patients, and healthcare institutions. Organised with rigorous planning and medical supervision, our drives ensure that hospitals across Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Surat, Nadiad, and surrounding regions have access to safe and sufficient blood components 24/7.

Beyond emergency blood collection, our events serve as vital platforms for public awareness. We actively engage youth, educational institutions, corporate organizations, and residential communities through interactive seminars, free health screening camps, and thalassemia education programs to foster a culture of voluntary, non-remunerated donation.

Every voluntary blood drive, medical camp, and awareness rally we host is powered by the passion of our self-less volunteers. Through transparency, compassionate service, and community participation, we continue to build trust and transform every single event into a life-saving celebration of humanity.
