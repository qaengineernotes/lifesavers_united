PRACTICAL GUIDANCE

WHAT TO DO WHILE WAITING FOR A DONOR

📞

Keep Your Phone Line Free

A coordinator will call from an official volunteer line to verify details. Ensure the contact number provided stays reachable.

📄

Prepare Doctor's Requisition

Keep the hospital blood requisition form, patient admission slip, and case number ready for quick verification.

🏥

Confirm if the patient's blood sample for cross-matching is already taken and verify the exact blood bank timings.

COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to common questions about submitting emergency blood requests and donor coordination.

❓ How quickly will a donor arrive after I submit a request?

For critical trauma and emergency cases, our coordinators initiate matching immediately. For most urgent requests, we aim to connect you with an available donor within 1 to 2 hours. Our network operates 24/7.

❓ Is there any charge or commission for requesting blood?

Strictly No. LifeSavers United is a non-profit voluntary initiative. Neither LifeSavers United nor our donors ever charge or accept money. Any request for money is strictly against our policies.

❓ Can I request specific blood components like SDP platelets or PRBC?

Yes. Our platform allows you to specify Single Donor Platelets (SDP), Packed Red Blood Cells (PRBC), Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP), or Whole Blood along with exact unit counts so we reach the right donors.

❓ What should I do if blood is arranged from somewhere else?

Please notify us immediately via our WhatsApp emergency line (9979260393). This helps our volunteers close the request and redirect standby donors to another waiting patient.

❓ Which cities does LifeSavers United cover?

We have active voluntary donor networks across Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot, Gandhinagar, Nadiad, Mumbai, and all major cities across Gujarat and India.
