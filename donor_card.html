Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about the LifeSavers United Hero Appreciation Card generator

🏆 Who can generate a Blood Donor Hero Appreciation Card?

Any registered donor, coordinator, or volunteer on LifeSavers United can generate these cards. If you are not yet registered, you can quickly register for free and verify your mobile number.

🔢 Can I specify my real lifetime donation number if not all are in the portal?

Yes! Many veteran donors have donated 25, 50, or 100+ times before joining LifeSavers United. You have complete freedom to write your true lifetime donation count (e.g., 103rd donation), and it will be rendered in 3D embossed gold on the plaque and medal.

📸 What photo works best for the tilted polaroid frame?

A clear portrait photo taken during or after your donation, holding your certificate, or resting post-donation works best. You can zoom and pan your photo within our studio to align it inside the tilted frame.

💬 Can I share my Hero Card directly to WhatsApp Status?

Yes! On mobile devices, tap "Share to WhatsApp / Social" to launch your device's native share sheet. On desktop, click "Download HD Card" to save a crisp 1254×1254 PNG image to post anywhere.

✨ Is this service completely free?

Yes. LifeSavers United is a 100% volunteer-run non-profit initiative. Every digital card, certificate, and portal feature is provided completely free to celebrate voluntary donors across India.
