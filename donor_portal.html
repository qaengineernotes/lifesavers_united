Why Join the Donor Portal

Most donors lose track of how many times they've donated or when they're next eligible. The LifeSavers United Donor Portal keeps that record for you. Log each donation — whether you find a donation camp near you, give at a hospital, or visit a blood bank — and we'll calculate your next eligible date automatically using NBTC/DGHS recovery guidelines (90 days for whole blood, 14 for platelets, 28 for plasma).

You'll also get a shareable digital donor ID card and a printable certificate of appreciation for every donation, so your contribution is recognized and easy to prove. Learn more about healthy recovery times and criteria in our blood compatibility and donation guides.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you navigate your voluntary blood donation portal

🩸 How do I log a blood donation on LifeSavers United?

Verify your registered mobile number with an OTP, then use the "Log a Donation" button inside the portal to add the date, donation type, and location. Coordinator-verified donations are locked for accuracy; self-reported entries can be edited later.

⏳ How do I check when I'm next eligible to donate blood?

Once logged in, your dashboard automatically shows your next eligible date for whole blood, platelets, and plasma, calculated from your last logged donation using DGHS and NBTC India recovery guidelines.

🪪 What is a digital donor card and how do I download mine?

Your digital donor card is a verified, shareable ID badge showing your blood group and donation milestones. After logging in, go to the "Digital Donor Card" tab and download it as a high-resolution image, or share it directly on WhatsApp.

🔒 Is my donation history verified by hospitals or self-reported?

Both. Donations made through LifeSavers United coordinator-organized camps or emergency requests are verified and locked. You can also self-report donations made independently, which you can edit or update anytime.

✨ I donated but I'm not registered yet — what do I do?

You'll need to register as a donor first. Register here, then return to the Donor Portal to verify your mobile number and log your donation.
